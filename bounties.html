BEFORE YOU SUBMIT

THE CLIP
IS THE PROOF.

Fortnite eliminations only. Keep it competitive and respectful.

What should my clip show?

Show your in-game name, the target's exact name, and the elimination clearly. Upload your clip to YouTube (unlisted is fine), Streamable, or Twitch Clips, then paste its link. Keep it available until review is complete.

When is a reward approved?

Submitting a clip does not reserve or award a bounty. An admin reviews the evidence and approves one winning claim per bounty. Denied claims include feedback and can be resubmitted while the bounty is open.

How do V-Bucks rewards work?

Bounties show a $5-$20 reward value in USD toward V-Bucks. Approval does not automatically deliver a reward. The WsB team arranges delivery with the winning member.

Claim details and clip links are visible to the submitting member and WsB admins. Video visibility is controlled by its hosting service.
