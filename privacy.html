Privacy & your profile.

WsB uses Google sign-in to connect a member to their profile. Signing in does not automatically grant membership or administrator access.

What is public?

Your preferred display name, bio, selected profile icon, and social links are public. Fortnite display names and statistics are public when available. Only add information and pictures you are comfortable sharing publicly.

What stays private?

Your invitation Gmail, Google account ID, and profile-access records are restricted to you and authorized WsB admins. Bounty claim links, review notes, dispute requests, and reward-delivery records are visible to the submitting member and admins. Administrative activity and encrypted backup exports are admin-only.

Clips and external services

WsB stores your clip link, not the video. YouTube, Streamable, Twitch, TikTok, Google sign-in, and Firebase operate under their own privacy policies. The video host controls who can watch your clip. Keep evidence available until your claim or dispute is resolved.

Control your information

Use My profile to update your public bio, socials, name, and icon. Admins manage Fortnite account identity, membership, roles, and linking. Contact a WsB admin through the community or Help & Contact to request unlinking, corrections, or removal of personal information.

Security and retention

Sign-in state is stored in your browser. Sign out on shared devices. We preserve bounty decisions and activity records to investigate mistakes and disputes; removing a bounty archives it rather than deleting its history. Private backups may retain earlier versions. Never submit passwords, recovery codes, or gift-card codes.

WsB does not sell profile or account-link information. This page describes the current setup and will be updated as features change.
