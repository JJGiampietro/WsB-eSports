QUESTIONS.

Quick answers about WsB, the site, joining the team, and keeping your profile updated.

Ask an admin to link your Gmail to your existing member profile. Then open My Profile and sign in with that exact Google account. The invitation message helps you get started; signing in with a different Gmail will not give access to the profile.

You can update your public display name, bio, profile icon, and supported social links without admin approval. Admins manage membership, roles, and the Fortnite account linked to your stats. Never share your password or Google verification codes.

A refresh can fail because of profile privacy, an account lookup issue, or a temporary service problem. Enable Public Game Stats in Fortnite and ask an admin to check your linked account. If an earlier successful snapshot is available, its date is shown. Missing data does not mean zero stats.

Use JOIN WsB to find the team contact options, or message @wsb.esports0 on TikTok about joining. We're always scouting for players who bring pressure, chemistry, and clutch plays.

Contact a WsB admin with your exact Fortnite username. They can add you to the roster, and the next successful hourly refresh will create your stats profile and save your Fortnite account ID. If you also want to edit your profile, give the admin the Gmail you will use to sign in.
